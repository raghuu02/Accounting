Questions

Good questions to ask.

Will we keep control of our clients?

Yes. You keep the client relationship, review and final approval, and your own processes and standards.

What work can you take off our hands?

Bookkeeping and reconciliations, payroll processing, month-end close support, and workpapers and document preparation.

How is work handed over?

Scope, owner and review points are agreed up front. Assigned people complete the work and raise exceptions early, and you review before anything is final.

Our protocols are designed to protect confidentiality and support compliance.
